About

Uzm. Dr. Umut İlhan

Dr. Umut İlhan is a specialist in pulmonology (chest diseases) at Yedikule Chest Diseases and Thoracic Surgery Education and Research Hospital in Istanbul — one of Turkey's premier referral centers for lung diseases, affiliated with the University of Health Sciences Turkey (Sağlık Bilimleri Üniversitesi).

His clinical and research focus centers on interstitial lung diseases (ILD), with particular expertise in advanced interventional bronchoscopy techniques including cryo-transbronchial biopsy (cryo-TBB) and bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL).

As an active researcher, Dr. İlhan has contributed as corresponding author and co-author to multiple peer-reviewed publications in leading international journals including BMC Pulmonary Medicine, Allergologia et Immunopathologia, Cureus, and Sarcoidosis, Vasculitis and Diffuse Lung Diseases.

Clinical Focus

Areas of Expertise

Specialized care in advanced pulmonary conditions with a focus on precision diagnostics and evidence-based treatment.

Interstitial Lung Diseases

Diagnosis and management of ILD including idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) and fibrosing ILD — his primary research and clinical focus.

Cryo-Transbronchial Biopsy

Advanced interventional bronchoscopy (cryo-TBB) for ILD diagnosis. Corresponding author of landmark study on cryo-TBB and BAL in BMC Pulmonary Medicine (2025).

Post-COVID-19 Lung Disease

Long-term immunosuppressive therapy management for post-COVID-19 interstitial lung disease, with published research on clinical, functional, and radiological outcomes.

Allergic Bronchopulmonary Aspergillosis

Stepwise and precision-based management of ABPA/ABPM and pulmonary aspergillosis, with multicenter real-life research contributions.

COPD & Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency

Genetic screening and management of alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency in COPD patients. National multicenter research published in Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences (2022).

Exercise Physiology in Lung Disease

Research on exercise-induced muscle oxygenation changes in fibrosing ILD using near-infrared spectroscopy — bridging functional and clinical pulmonology.
